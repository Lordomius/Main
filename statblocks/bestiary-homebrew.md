___
>## Elmin Ilvrost
>*Medium Humanoid (Elf, Drow), Neutral Evil*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 20 (*robe of the archmagi*, *staff of power*); 25 with *shield*
>- **Hit Points** 340 (40d8 + 160)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +3 (13)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|8 (-1)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|22 (+6)|14 (+2)|20 (+5)|
>___
>- **Saving Throws** Str +1, Dex +5, Con +14, Int +16, Wis +12, Cha +7
>- **Skills** Arcana +14, Deception +13, Insight +10, Perception +10, Persuasion +13
>- **Damage Immunities** psychic (while *mind blank* is active)
>- **Condition Immunities** charmed (while *mind blank* is active)
>- **Senses** superior darkvision 120 ft., truesight 120 ft. (while *true seeing* is active), passive Perception 20
>- **Languages** Common, Elvish, Undercommon, Abyssal, Deep Speech, Draconic
>- **Challenge** 25 (75,000 XP)
>___
>***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If Elmin fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
>
>***Greater Portent.*** When Elmin finishes a long rest, he rolls three d20s and records the numbers. He can replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by himself or a creature he can see with one of these foretelling rolls. He must choose to do so before the roll, and he can do so only once per turn. Each foretelling roll can be used only once.
>
>***Expert Divination.*** When Elmin casts a divination spell of 2nd level or higher using a spell slot, he regains one expended spell slot. The slot must be of a level lower than the spell he cast and can't be higher than 5th level.
>
>***Contingency.*** When Elmin is reduced to half his hit points or fewer, or becomes grappled or restrained, *dimension door* takes effect on him immediately, carrying him to a location he chose when casting *contingency*.
>
>***Fey Ancestry.*** Elmin has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put him to sleep.
>
>***Magic Resistance.*** Elmin has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects (*robe of the archmagi*).
>
>***Foresight.*** While the spell is active, Elmin can't be surprised and has advantage on attack rolls, ability checks (including initiative), and saving throws, and other creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls against him.
>
>***War Caster.*** Elmin has advantage on Constitution saving throws made to maintain concentration, and he can perform the somatic components of spells even when his hands are full.
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Elmin is a 20th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 24, +18 to hit with spell attacks). He can cast *shield* and *misty step* at will without expending a spell slot (Spell Mastery), and he can cast *counterspell* and *dispel magic* once each at 3rd level without expending a spell slot, regaining the ability to do so when he finishes a short or long rest (Signature Spells). He has the following wizard spells prepared (Elmin casts the spells marked † on himself before combat; the slots listed are those he has remaining after these daily preparations):
>
>Cantrips (at will): *Fire Bolt*, *Mage Hand*, *Mind Sliver*, *Minor Illusion*, *Toll the Dead*
>1st level (4 slots): *Absorb Elements*, *Magic Missile*, *Shield*, *Silvery Barbs*
>2nd level (3 slots): *Mirror Image*, *Misty Step*, *See Invisibility*
>3rd level (3 slots): *Counterspell*, *Dispel Magic*, *Fireball*, *Slow*, *Thunder Step*
>4th level (3 slots): *Banishment*, *Dimension Door*, *Greater Invisibility*
>5th level (3 slots): *Scrying*, *Synaptic Static*, *Wall of Force*
>6th level (2 slots): *Contingency*†, *Disintegrate*, *True Seeing*†
>7th level (2 slots): *Forcecage*, *Prismatic Spray*
>8th level (1 slot): *Mind Blank*†, *Power Word Stun*
>9th level (1 slot): *Foresight*†, *Meteor Swarm*, *Wish*
>
>***Innate Spellcasting.*** Elmin's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 21). He can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
>
>At will: *Dancing Lights*
>1/day each: *Darkness*, *Faerie Fire*
>
>### Actions
>***Staff of Power.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage, plus 3 (1d6) force damage if Elmin expends 1 charge.
>
>***Fire Bolt (Cantrip).*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +18 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (4d10) fire damage.
>
>***Toll the Dead (Cantrip).*** One creature Elmin can see within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw or take 18 (4d8) necrotic damage, or 26 (4d12) necrotic damage if it is missing any hit points.
>
>***Spellcasting.*** Elmin casts one of his prepared spells or one of the spells stored in his *staff of power*.
>
>### Reactions
>***Spell Reactions.*** Elmin can use his reaction to cast *shield*, *absorb elements*, *silvery barbs*, or *counterspell*.
>
>### Legendary Actions
>*Elmin can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Elmin regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.*
>
>- **Cantrip.** Elmin casts a cantrip.
>- **Foreseen Step.** Elmin teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see.
>- **Doom Sight (Costs 2 Actions).** Elmin shows one creature he can see within 120 feet a vision of its own death. The target must make a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 36 (8d8) psychic damage and has disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and suffers no other effect.
>- **Quickened Prophecy (Costs 3 Actions).** Elmin casts a prepared spell of 5th level or lower, expending a spell slot as normal.
